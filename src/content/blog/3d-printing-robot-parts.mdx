## Beyond Prototyping

3D printing isn't just for mockups. With the right design practices, printed parts can serve as functional components in robots that run for thousands of hours.

## Material Selection

- **PLA** — Easy to print, dimensionally accurate, but brittle under impact
- **PETG** — Good balance of strength, flexibility, and printability
- **Nylon** — Excellent wear resistance for gears and bearings
- **ASA** — UV-stable for outdoor robots

## Design for Print

Key rules:
1. Orient parts so layer lines don't align with primary stress directions
2. Use chamfers instead of fillets on bottom edges
3. Design snap-fit features with 0.3mm clearance
4. Add mouse ears to corners prone to warping

## Structural Optimization

Topology optimization in Fusion 360 or SolidWorks can reduce material use by 40-60% while maintaining strength. The organic shapes it produces are perfect for additive manufacturing.

## Heat Set Inserts

For threaded connections, heat-set brass inserts are far superior to threading directly into plastic. They provide reliable clamping force and can be assembled/disassembled repeatedly.
