## Power Budget First

Before selecting batteries, calculate your power budget. List every subsystem with its voltage, peak current, and duty cycle.

| Subsystem | Voltage | Peak Current | Avg Current |
|-----------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Drive motors (x4) | 12V | 5A each | 2A each |
| Compute (Jetson) | 5V | 4A | 2.5A |
| Sensors | 3.3V | 0.5A | 0.3A |
| Servos (x6) | 7.4V | 2A each | 0.5A each |

## Battery Selection

LiPo batteries offer the best energy density. For a 1-hour runtime with the budget above, you need approximately 200Wh — a 6S 8000mAh pack.

## Voltage Regulation

Use synchronous buck converters (not linear regulators) for efficiency. The TPS54560 handles 5A continuous with >90% efficiency across a wide input range.

## Safety

- Always include a main power switch rated for your peak current
- Add reverse polarity protection (P-channel MOSFET, not a diode)
- Include voltage monitoring to warn before the battery hits minimum voltage
- Fuse each high-current branch independently
